# Project Soulwarden

Soulwarden runs the last retail New World: Aeternum client against a server on your own computer. No public service is involved. The game talks to a local backend and to nothing else.

It is early. You can create a character, walk and travel in the whole world, put on armor, and fight the creatures of the world with melee attacks, with the basic attacks of the bow, the musket, and the magic staffs and gauntlets, with the abilities of the great axe and the war hammer, and with the Ice Pylon, the musket trap, and the Beacon of the life staff: the creatures patrol, see you, attack, shoot arrows and cast spells at you (the archers and the mages), and go back to their place. Some creatures cast areas on the ground: a poison cloud, a lightning strike, a fire pillar, a plague nova around a ghost, the shockwave of a knight. Some heal or buff themselves: an Inquisitor Cleric heals itself at low health, an Evil Knight hound howls and bites harder. You can dodge.

It supports one client build, `NewWorld.exe` 1.400.6031.6004151 from Steam app 1063730, on Linux with Proton. The SHA-256 of that file is `8654f01d324636d9f74f1c793b0cc4a417c3c5fa9847d9913c358ca29e0fdc8e`.

This is an independent project with no connection to Amazon Games. The repository has no game files, so you need your own copy of the game.

## What works

- Login with the Steam account of the computer. Each account has its own characters.
- Character creation and deletion, in the screens of the client.
- The world. Your character enters, moves, and comes back at the exact place where it stood.
- The bag, the coin, and equipment. Armor and weapons show on the body. The character list of the main menu shows each character with the armor that it wears (head, chest, hands, legs, feet), also after a change in the world. The menu shows no weapon, as in the original game.
- Level, attributes, weapon mastery trees, the ability bar, and trade skills.
- The full game with its DLC, for each account and each character, with no purchase and no setting: the expansion Rise of the Angry Earth and the DLC packs of the game. Your character goes up to level 70 (a level 60 character keeps its level and experience and goes on with experience), the trade skills go up to 250 (also Riding), the flail tree opens with its normal points, and items up to gear score 800 can be equipped (with their level). An artifact can have a gear score up to 800 (the upgrade limit of the game data). Food of level 65 works at level 65. Each character gets the amulet of a DLC pack (AmuletIsabellaT1) one time. The content of the expansion (the region Elysian Wilds, mounts, expeditions, its quests) is not there yet, and the skins, emotes, and crests of the DLC packs need their systems.
- Creatures at their real spawn points in the whole world, 33,023 points and 3,901 kinds in 25 regions.
- Melee combat with a sword, a rapier, a hatchet, a flail, a spear, a great axe, a war hammer, or a greatsword. A hit shows its damage number, and creatures die. Each swing of a chain has its own damage as in the game: the last swing of a chain, the quick double thrust of the rapier, a heavy attack that you release early, and a full charge (hold the button for 0.67 s) each do their own damage.
- The twelve abilities of the great axe (Charge, Maelstrom, Gravity Well, Reap, Execute, Whirlwind) and of the war hammer (Wrecking Ball, Shockwave, Path of Destiny, Armor Breaker, Mighty Gavel, Clear Out), with the upgrades of their trees. Reap pulls the creatures in its reach to you, Clear Out pushes them away, Whirlwind with Unending Winds spins on while it hits, Execute does more damage to a creature below half health, Armor Breaker ignores a part of the armor, Charge swings with the right button (Unpredictable Strike), and Clear Out with Power Cleaner fortifies you and your friends near you. An ability does its damage, its button on the HUD shows the cooldown, and a creature takes the stun, the root, the slow, the knockdown, or the weaken of the ability, with its icon at the name plate. The vortex of Gravity Well shows on the ground and bursts after 3 s with its damage numbers; another player near it sees the same vortex. Put the nodes and the bar in the Weapon Mastery window (K).
- Pulls and pushes. Maelstrom pulls the creatures around you to you, the vortex of Gravity Well pulls the creatures in it to its center and its burst pushes them out, and Path of Destiny, Shockwave, Charge, and the light and heavy attacks of the melee weapons push a creature back, with the distances of the game data. A wall, a rock, or an edge stops a creature that a hit moves. The dash of Charge goes up to 14.7 m, and another player sees your character run in the dash.
- Bows, muskets, staffs, and gauntlets. The basic attacks of the bow (a hip shot, an aimed shot, a charged shot) and of the fire staff fly on the server with the speed, the gravity, and the range of the game data, and do their damage when they hit a creature, about 0.1 s for 10 m. The musket, the life staff, the ice gauntlet, and the void gauntlet use the same rules with their data (not yet tested in the game). A wall or the ground stops a shot: the creature behind it takes nothing. A shot that misses does nothing. No arrows are necessary (the game does not ask for them).
- The Ice Pylon of the ice gauntlet, the trap of the musket, and the Beacon of the life staff (key Q with the ability on the bar). The Pylon stands 2 m in front of you for 15 s, shoots the creatures near it each 2 s, and a creature that it hits attacks the Pylon (its name plate and health bar show); it costs 15 mana when it comes; while it lives a second press does nothing (the button shows no countdown then), and its cooldown of 10 s starts when it ends. The trap opens where it lands and closes on the first creature that walks into it: damage and a root of 3 s; with no creature it goes away after 30 s. The Beacon sticks to the creature or the player that it hits (it hurts a creature), follows it for 10 s, and gives you its buff and a heal each second while you stand in 3 m of it. Another player near them sees the same Pylon (with no name plate), trap, and Beacon.
- Riposte of the rapier: a creature that hits you in the 1 s after the press takes the counter of the server (its damage, a stun of 2 s, and a push of 1.5 m), one time for each press.
- A haste on your character makes your run and your sprint faster for its time (the Detonate heart rune: 1.2 times for 3 s).
- Creatures fight. A creature sees your character, runs to it with its run animation, attacks, and goes back to its place at the end of its leash. 3,144 of the 3,901 kinds of the spawn points attack (751 kinds and the six kinds of the totems and the nests do not).
- Archers shoot. The archers of the game (the Varangian, Lugdunum, and mercenary archers, the skeleton archers, the Dryad archers, and the Damned bowmen) draw the bow, release at the time of the game data, and shoot one arrow that flies on the server and that each player sees. You lose health only when the arrow touches you: step to the side at the release and it misses, and a wall or the ground stops it. An archer shoots from up to 20 m each 3 s, walks back a few meters when you come very near, and uses its melee attack when you are in its reach. The Varangian archers about 450 m to the west of the default spawn show it.
- Mages cast. The Skeleton Mages, the Ancient Guardian mages, the Dynasty summoners, the Dryad shamans, and the Damned priests raise their hands, cast one ball at the time of the game data, and the ball flies on the server to you. A mage casts from up to 16 m each 2.5 s and walks back a few meters when you come nearer than 6 m.
- Creatures cast areas on the ground. The Dryad shamans put poison mushrooms at your feet: a small green glow, then a green cloud for 26 s. In the cloud you get the poison (its icon on the HUD) and lose health each 2 s; step out and the poison ends. The Undead shamans call a lightning strike: a dark ring on the ground, then the bolt 0.9 s later, one hit if you are in the ring. The Ancient Guardian mages (a purple area that slows and weakens you), the Inquisitor clerics (a fire pillar), the named Damned priests, and the Soul Harvesters do the same. A dodge in the right moment avoids a strike; the strike of a shaman ends when the shaman dies, the mushrooms stay. A creature casts its area about each 10 s (an Undead shaman each 5 s) and shoots or hits between. The Plagued ghosts, the Corrupted Legion signifers, the undead officers, and the war hammer knights cast their area around themselves when you are near them (the plague nova, the corrupted blast, the anchor slam, the shockwave of the knight in the direction that it looks).
- Creatures heal and buff themselves. An Inquisitor Cleric at 15 percent of its health or less heals itself: 4.5 percent each second for 8 seconds, with the heal icon at its name plate, and its health bar goes up. An Evil Knight hound howls when it sees you: for 10 seconds it does 10 percent more damage and runs 20 percent faster, with the icon at its name plate. Each one does it again 20 seconds later at most. The heal and the buff end at their time and at the death of the creature.
- Dodge and stamina. Press left Shift to dodge. The server keeps your stamina with the values of the game data: a dodge costs 45 (50 in heavy armor), and for 0.5 s a creature hit does nothing to you: an arrow or a ball flies through you. Your stamina comes back 33 in each second, starting 1 s after the dodge. At 0 you cannot dodge until your stamina is full again. The shield of Maelstrom with Absorb takes the arrows and the balls of the creatures.
- Creatures find their way around walls and rocks, up stairs, and on the floors of buildings and ruins.
- Creatures walk their patrol routes from the game files, or wander near their place. After a fight a creature runs back to the place where it saw you. On that way it is immune and walks the way that it came, and at that place it has its full health and patrols again.
- Health and death. Your character loses health and dies, and it comes back at the nearest spirit shrine or inn.
- Rewards of a kill. A creature that you kill gives experience and weapon mastery experience, sometimes coin, and sometimes loot from the loot tables of the game, with the notices of the HUD. A loot item gets its perks with the perk roll of the game. A creature of a spawn point comes back after its respawn time, 6 minutes for most of them.
- Loot bags. The loot of a kill goes into a loot bag at the place of the creature, with a light beam of the color of its best item. Only you see your bag; each character with a part of the kill has its own bag content. Press E at the bag to take all, or T (or Tab near the bag) to open its panel: take one item with LSHIFT and a click, a part of a stack with LCTRL and a click, or all with Take All. When your bag is full, you take what fits and the rest stays. The bag goes away 3 minutes after the kill, or 1.25 s after you took the last item. An item that you drag to "Drop items" (also an equipped item) goes into a bag at your feet, and you can take it back for 3 minutes. A quest item, and each other item that the game does not let you drop, stays in your bag.
- Mana. Your maximum mana has the bonus of your Focus (Focus 100 or more: 110) and the bonuses of your perks and effects. After a spell the mana bar below the crosshair fills again while your mana comes back, and it goes away at full mana.
- Day and night. The world has one day and night cycle for each player: 30 minutes of real time from 06:00 to 19:00, and 15 minutes of night, as in the game. The sky, the sun, the moon, the light, and the shadows follow it, and the time goes on after a restart from where it stopped (it does not move while the server is stopped; a new world starts at noon). Some creatures of the world come only at night or only by day, and they go away at the end of their time (for example the ghosts near the lake of Brightwood Isle come at night and go at 06:26). The rain, the snow, the fog, and the light of caves and dungeons show at their places, as before. The tab "World" of the tool window shows the time and sets it for the whole world.
- Item perks and gems. An item has up to five perks; the tooltip shows them, the gem, the gear score, and the rarity. The perks of your gear give their values: health, armor, absorption, damage, the gem conversion, the regeneration, and the perks of a hit (Sundering Shockwave, Insatiable Gravity Well, Refreshing Move, the void perks). The equip load changes your damage.
- Potions, food, and dust. The quick slot keys (3 to 6) use the item of the slot: it heals, gives its effect, and starts its cooldown, with the countdown on the slot. The other potions of the same kind get 80 percent of the cooldown. An item above the level of your character does nothing. The HUD shows the icon of the effect with its countdown, and the character sheet shows the attributes of a food and the armor of a dust.
- The heart rune. Hits fill the charge of the heart rune (key G). At a full charge, G starts Detonate: its icon and its effect show on your character, and 3 seconds later it hurts each creature within 5 m.
- Critical hits. Each hit can be critical with the critical chance of your weapon and of your nodes, perks, and effects; a critical hit shows the orange damage number and does the critical damage of the weapon. A hit from behind a creature (135 degrees or more from its front, for a wolf) and an arrow or a shot in the head of a creature are always critical.
- The passive nodes of the great axe and the war hammer, from the rows of the game data: the effects on your own character after a hit or when creatures are near you (Mauler's Fury, Greed, Hammer Time, Safety Measures, Center of Attention), the effects on the creature (the exhaust of Exhaustive Attacks, the slow of Aftershock), the shorter cooldowns, the heals of a part of the damage, the armor penetration, and the critical nodes. In a development run, `tools/world passives NAME` lists each node with what the backend does with it. Another player sees the icons of your public effects at your name plate.
- The heals of the life staff. Divine Embrace (its key on the bar, then LCTRL for yourself or a click for a friend) heals after its cast of 1 second; Sacred Ground (its key, then LCTRL or a click) puts a holy area at your feet that heals you each second for 12 seconds; the Beacon heals each second. Your heals scale with the base damage of your staff, its gear score, and your Focus. You see a green number for each heal, and a green number at the feet of a friend that you heal. Effects that lower healing (Disease) and the heal nodes (Divine Blessing: more healing on a target below half health; Anointed) count. Potions, food, and the life steal of a perk heal through the same rules.
- Chests. Press E at a supply cache, a provisions crate, an alchemy chest, or an elite supply chest of the world and hold still while the ring of the prompt runs (2.5 s; 5 s for an elite chest): you get the loot of its loot table, with the gear score of its place and the notice of the HUD. Each character has its own loot: the chest stays looted for your character (no prompt) for 60 minutes, and an elite chest for 23 hours, also after a restart, and a different character can open it. A walk, a hit, or a release of E before the end gives nothing. When your bag has no place for the whole loot, the chest gives nothing and stays closed: make space and open it again, and you get the same loot. A chest with a key, a lock, or a quest has no prompt.
- Gathering: mining, logging, harvesting, and skinning. Put the tool of a node into its slot of the paperdoll (the pick, the axe, the sickle, the skinning knife; a tool in the bag does not count, and the prompt says "No equipped Pick"), press E at an ore vein, a boulder, a stone, a tree, a plant, a bush, or the body of a creature with skinning (a rabbit, a wolf), and hold still while the ring of the prompt runs. You get the materials of the loot table of the node and the trade skill experience of the game, with the notices of the HUD; a tree falls, and the node goes away for each player near it. It comes back after its respawn time from the game data (for example 7 to 10 minutes for a young tree), also after a restart (the time does not run while the server is stopped). The time of the ring comes from the gathering speed of your tool, of your trade skill rank, and of your effects; your rank and your luck effects make the rare finds of a node more likely. A node of a rank above yours gives nothing. A walk, a hit, or a release of E before the end gives nothing. When your bag has no place for the whole result, the node gives nothing and stays: make space and gather it again, and you get the same materials. Two players can work on one node: the first one that ends gets it. 2.45 million natural nodes and 33,071 placed nodes of the world work.
- Lore pages. Press E (Read) at a lore page: the journal opens on the page, and the page stays in the journal of your character, also after a restart and on the next day. T (Add To Journal) adds the page with no read. The prompt then shows "Already Read". The server checks each use of an object (the object, its option, your distance, and its limits) with the data of the game: 44,534 objects of the world (1,157 lore pages, 1,455 persons, gathering nodes, shrines, doors) are in the interaction data.
- Talks with the persons of the world (stage 2.5c), and the sit and pet animations of the objects that have them (a chair takes one character at a time). Each person of the game is there: Aelstrom, Urda, and Grace on the beach of Windsward, the persons of the settlements (also the artificer at the forge of Oxboro), and the persons of the quests. A person of a quest is there for your character as the game shows it: a person that your progress hides is not there for you (E does nothing), and it comes back when the progress shows it again; another player sees the persons of its own progress. Press E: the conversation shows the text of the state of your character, and the topics of your active quest tasks are offered and accepted. An innkeeper shows the Inn option (no inn screen yet).
- The crafting stations of the settlements. Each settlement has its stations at their places (for example the forge, the workshop, and the woodshop of Oxboro in Everfall): go into the station, and press E (Craft) at its prompt ("WORKSHOP TIER 5"). The crafting screen of the game opens with your recipes.
- Crafting of the fixed recipes (stage 3.7a): the flint tools (Flint Skinning Knife, Flint Logging Axe, Flint Mining Pick, Flint Harvesting Sickle), the fishing pole, and the wood sword. At the workshop (Engineering) or the forge (Blacksmith), select the recipe, set the count, and press CRAFT: about 1 s later the item is in your bag (a knife has gear score 100), the materials of the recipe are gone, and the fee of the station is paid (0.01 coin). A count of 3 shows "3 ITEMS CRAFTED". A craft stays after a restart. With no materials the screen shows "Crafting attempt failed." after 10 s, and nothing changes. The stations with no row of their tier in the game data (5 woodshops, smelters, tanneries, looms, and stonecutting tables) use the next tier ("WOODSHOP TIER 3" in Oxboro). The recipes with an unlock are refused yet.
- Refining and the reward progress (stage 3.7b): the refining recipes (Iron Ingot at the smelter, Timber at the woodshop, Coarse Leather at the tannery, Linen at the loom, Stone Block at the stonecutting table, and the higher tiers with their reagents, for example Steel Ingot with Obsidian Flux and Charcoal), the food, the potions, the jewelry, and the furniture with a fixed result: 948 recipes. Each craft gives the trade skill experience of the game ("+44 Smelting" for an iron ingot); the first craft of a recipe gives three times that one time ("First Craft Bonus Available", "+132 Smelting"), also after a restart. The "Chance to craft additional items" of the screen gives bonus items: the result screen shows TOTAL, BASE AMOUNT, and BONUS (for example 25% at Smelting 200). Some recipes (Asmodeum and the other tier 5 refines) have a cooldown: "Crafting Cooldown: 23h 59m per 9 items" after one craft; the time counts while the server runs, also after a restart. The trade skill experience is the number of the screen also for a reward with a fraction.
- Crafted gear with a gear score and perks (stage 3.7c): the weapons, the armor, the jewelry, the tools, and the bags of the game (837 procedural recipes; 1,917 recipes in all). The gear score is the number of the screen: the base of the recipe, your trade skill rank, the materials that you select (Prismatic Ingot +20), and your food, gear, and trophy bonuses (for example the Weaponsmith food +15; at most +50). An Orichalcum Great Axe with Prismatic materials, Weaponsmithing 200, and the food has 600. The perks come from the rules of the game: put a charm in a "Craft Mod" slot (a Reinforced Starmetal Great Axe Charm gives Enfeebling Maelstrom; the Gem Setting Pins give a gem slot), and add Azoth (the "Azoth" slot: 8, 16, 24, 33, or 41 Azoth at a tier 5 station) to make more perks certain. The result screen and the tooltip show the gear score, the rarity (5 perks: LEGENDARY), and the perks; the item stays after a restart. Recipes with Azoth as a material (the Matrix components, the Prismatic jewelry parts) take it from your Azoth. Your Azoth shows in the crafting screen and in the bag (cap 2,500); set it in the tool window (tab Character, row Azoth): the game has no Azoth source yet.
- Quests, first part (stage 1.8a). A new character starts on the beach of Windsward with the main story quest "Soul Survivor": the HUD shows it pinned with its task "Talk to Aelstrom on the beach", a marker at the place of the task, and the quest in the journal (key J) with its rewards. The quest, its tasks, and its pin stay after a restart. The quests that you pin or unpin in the journal stay pinned or unpinned. An existing character gets no quest by itself; the admin command `apply_backstory` (or `tools/world quest template` in a development run) gives it the story position of one backstory template of the game (its achievements and its start quest, not its level or items): for example `Level60_Brimstone_Unlocked` gives "Brimstone Under Siege" (`tools/world quest templates` lists them).
- Quests, second part (stage 1.8b). The persons give and take the quests. A new character starts the beach quest "Soul Survivor": the beach cutscene plays by itself, and when you talk to Aelstrom, "Complete the Quest!" ends the quest with its rewards (200 experience, 1.50 coin, and the next part of the story); Aelstrom then offers the next quest, and you accept it in the talk. A quest whose tasks are done waits for its hand-in at the person of the quest marker. The trigger areas of the beach, the cutscenes of the quests, the whispers (the voice and the subtitles of a whisper), and the effects of a topic (a person who walks away after the talk) work. A hand-in is refused, and the quest stays ready, when your bag is full or when the quest gives a reward that the server cannot pay (the list is in the fourth part).
- Quests, third part (stage 1.8c). The tasks of your quests count what you do: a kill (each creature category of the task: "Kill 5 Corrupted" counts the corrupted sailors, and only a kill for which you have the credit), the items in your bag (a task to have or to bring an item counts them, also again when they leave the bag), an item that you equip, a gather (the quest objects such as the treasures of the shipwrecks show their prompt and their light only while you have their task, and each character gathers its own), the quest items that a kill or a gather drops for a task, a craft or a refine, the places (enter or leave a point of interest such as a shipwreck or the watchtower), and the screens that a task wants you to open. The HUD shows the counter of a task ("1/5") and the next task at once. In the journal you can reset a main story quest (it starts again, with no second copy of an item that it gives one time) and abandon the quests that the game lets you abandon; the quest items of the quest go back. The tasks of a person, a cutscene, or a trigger of the beach count from stage 1.8b.
- Quests, fourth part (stage 1.8d). A quest pays all its rewards when you hand it in: the experience, the coin, the items (with their perks), the territory standing of the territory of the quest (the map shows it, for example "120 / 150" for Windsward), and the next part of the story. A Meat Pouch ("Essential Supplies Cache") is a reward box: in the bag (Tab), select it and "Open", and you get its items with the reward popup of the game (5 Weak Health Potions and 10 Travel Rations). A person offers a quest only when your character can take it, as in the game (the level, the earlier quests, the archetype); a daily or weekly quest comes again after the reset of the game (05:00 UTC; weekly on Tuesday). The beach chain now plays from the start: craft the skinning knife and cook the Light Ration at Urda's Campfire in the watchtower camp (E "Craft"), skin a boar, and help your crewmates at the shipwreck. It stops at "Amidst the Wreckage" (`06_survivors`): the corruption nodules near Grace cannot be destroyed yet.
- Quests, fifth part (stage 1.8e). The whole beach chain plays from the start to the town of Corinth: hit the corruption nodules near Grace (four hits each; they go for your character only, and they come back after half a minute), recover Grace's treasures and her flag and wheel, destroy the Corruption Nexus in the Old Tidal Cave with one hit, and bring the warning to Marcel Dupont in Corinth (800 experience, 6.00 coin, an amulet, and 120 Windsward standing). The side quest "Truffle Fever" of Hapless Howe works too (the sickle, five truffles, the hand-in in Corinth). A quest with an Azoth reward pays it into your Azoth balance (to 2,500).
- Quests, small rules (stage QG2a). A kill counts for the group of its creature too: a Bronzeroot Soldier counts for "Destroy Bronzeroot" (the creature category of its place and the group of that category). A task "reach level N" completes at that level, also when you reach it later. The coin purses and coin stacks of the quests ("Bag of Coin") open in the bag (double click, or "Open"): the coin goes into your wallet, and the purse goes. A quest with an Azoth reward of 37.5 in the game data pays 37, the amount that the journal and the hand-in show. The quests of a story arc are offered as the game offers them (the arc of a quest is no condition). Some quests come by themselves when your character can take them ("pushed"): at the world entry and when you complete a quest or reach a level, for example "Faction Introductions" at level 17, "Acquire a Mount" at level 20, "Learn to Craft" after the market of Windsward, and "Orbs of Power" at level 60; an existing character gets them at its next entry. A pushed quest that you abandon comes again (its condition is true again).
- Quest places (stage QG3). More places of the game count for the quest tasks of a place: the areas of each territory (for example "Bragging Rights" of Everfall completes when you return to Faith's Bounty, and the bears of "Grizzly Neighbors" count in Greyvale), and the points of interest whose place the earlier data did not find (for example Oxboro for "The Defense of Everfall", the watchtower and the shipwrecks of the Everfall coast, the shrines of Ebonscale Reach, and the legion camps of Brimstone Sands). 787 places of the open world have their shape now (476 before). A trigger area of a dungeon or of an introduction world never counts in the open world. In a development run, `tools/world quest places CHARACTER` shows the places of the data where your character stands.
- Quest givers (stage QG4). The persons of the town of Grayvale in Mourningdale (for example Constable Martin Bayles: "When it Rains, It Pours") and of the town of East Consolation in Restless Shores are there and give their quests (13 more quests of these persons). A person of the story who stands in a cage or behind a door of the story (for example Nessa Harrower in her cage near Buccaneer Creek in Windsward) is there for your character as the game shows it: when your progress opens the place, the person comes. An artifact item gives its artifact quest when you equip it at the level of the item (level 65 for gear score 700): the journal shows it in "Artifacts" with its tasks. An artifact that you had equipped before gives its quest only at the next equip. The completion of an artifact quest does not work yet. The persons of a dungeon, of the old introduction world, and of a season are not in the open world.
- Quest chests and quest objects (stage QG5a). A quest that asks for an item "from chests" gives it when you open a chest at the place of the quest: for example "Earth Will Provide" gives Alkahest from the chests of Postrekhin in Brightwood (one item for each chest, with the loot of the chest and its notice, until the quest has its count; a chest at a different place gives none). The trails and the torches of the quests count when you walk over them: for example the wolf tracks of "No Good Deed" in Everfall (each track of your active task, one after the other; the client shows its effect, and the next track comes). The glyph locks of Brimstone Sands open with your Azoth Staff (in its slot, with the rank of the staff and the glyph of the lore page). More quest objects of the world work: the trapped animals and the captives that you free (for example "Endangered by Artemis"), the graves, murals, and spheres of the main story, and the objects of a quest whose look comes from its variant (for example the poem of the Empress in Ebonscale Reach). A quest object that you used goes away for your character and comes back after a time of the game data; each character has its own. A new character completes 66 quests of the support list now.
- The quest persons of Nighthaven, the items of a quest object, and the Azoth Staff ranks (stage QG5b). A person whose place depends on the step of a quest shows for your character at that step: for example the caged Cecilia in Nighthaven is in her cage while your character has "The Wolf's Den" and has not freed her (211 persons of Nighthaven and other places). A quest object that needs an item works when the item is in your bag: for example the Altar of the Departed of "A Flower for the Departed" in Ebonscale Reach takes the Blossoming Flower (the item stays in your bag). If the item comes into your bag while you stand at the object, walk away and back: then the client starts the gather. The main story quests that give the Azoth Staff also give its rank (for example "A Time For Peace, A Time for War" gives one rank), and the glyph locks open with that rank. ## How it works

Two programs come out of this tree.

The hook replaces `steam_api64.dll`, and the game loads it at its start. It gives the client its Steam identity, sends every HTTP request of the client to the local backend, blocks all traffic that is not loopback, and makes the client trust the certificate of the backend. It also draws the tool window, and it changes 6 bytes of client code so that the client reports the exact position of your character.

The backend is a C++ service that listens on loopback only. It answers the HTTP calls of the login path and runs the game server, which is DTLS 1.2, a datagram carrier, and state replication. One thread owns the world. Accounts, characters, items, and progress are in a SQLite file in `~/.local/share/soulwarden`.

The launcher starts the game in a network namespace that has loopback and no other interface. From there the game cannot reach the internet, with or without the hook.

```sh
tools/gamedata/conversations.sh      # the conversation data of the persons (stage 2.5c)

linux-compat/run-soulwarden.sh
```

At the main menu, select a character and press Play. `tools/release.sh` makes a release, and `tools/steam-shortcut.py add` puts the launcher of the current release in your Steam library.

### Releases

The Steam library entry starts the current release. A release is a fixed copy of the launcher, the backend, the hook, and the game data of a tested state, in `~/Games/soulwarden/releases`. A new build or a change of this tree does not change your game. A new release does.

```sh
tools/release.sh                     # make a release of the tested commit, and make it current
tools/release.sh status              # the current release (*) and the other releases
tools/release.sh rollback            # go back to the release before the current one
tools/release.sh activate VERSION    # go to a different release
tools/steam-shortcut.py add          # with Steam closed: the entry starts the current release
```

`cat ~/Games/soulwarden/releases/current/VERSION` shows the version of the game that the entry starts. While the game runs, the release command does not change the current release: do `tools/release.sh activate VERSION` after the game stops. The command keeps the 3 newest releases and the current one. `tools/steam-shortcut.py add --launcher working-copy` makes the entry start the launcher of this tree.

## The tool window

Press F8 in the world. The window has six tabs for items, the character, skills, creatures, travel, and the world. It gives items, levels, and skills at once; in the world only kills give them yet.

The tab "Items" gives an item with a gear score and with perks: type the PerkID texts with "," between them (for example `PerkID_Stat_TwoHandSoldier,PerkID_Weapon_CDRBasic,PerkID_Gem_VoidDMG4`; a gem is a perk).

The tab "Character" shows your health. "Heal" gives your character its full health, and it makes a dead character live again at its place. With the mark "Creatures ignore this character", no creature attacks you, and your kills give nothing. With the mark "Each kill drops loot and coin", each kill of your character drops loot and coin (without it, a wolf drops loot in about 1 of 10 kills). `SOULWARDEN_CREATURE_BEHAVIOR=0` starts the game with creatures that stand still. After you come into the world, travel, or respawn, no creature attacks you for 10 seconds unless you hit it. Out of a fight, your health comes back in about 30 seconds.

At the end of the tab "Character" (scroll down) the group "Build presets" gives your character a whole build in one step. Choose the preset in the list, choose "PvE tree" or "PvP tree", press "Apply", read the line "This replaces the equipment, the level, the attributes, and the trees of CHARACTER.", and press "Confirm" (or "Cancel"). The line below then says "done" with the number of changes, or why the server refused it in red. The items that your character had in those slots go into the bag; when the bag has no room for them, nothing changes. A second "Apply" changes only what is different: with the other tree, only the bow tree changes. Your earlier trees, level, and attributes are not kept.

The tab "Travel" lists the spirit shrines and the inns of each territory. Type a part of a name in the filter, click a place, and press "Travel". Your character is there after a loading screen of some seconds, with the creatures of that place around it.

The tab "World" shows the time of the world and its phase (night, dawn, day, dusk). Set the hour and the minute and press "Set", or press 06:00, 12:00, 19:00, or 00:00: the sky changes at once, for each player of the world.

While the window is open, the keyboard and the pointer go to it and not to the game. `SOULWARDEN_TOOL_WINDOW=0` starts the game without it.

## Identity and safety

The launcher reads your Steam id and your persona name from `config/loginusers.vdf` of the local Steam installation. It reads no Steam credential, and it does not start or contact Steam.

Authentication is a stub. The backend accepts the Steam id that the local ticket names, so any process on your computer can log in as any account. That is acceptable on loopback and would be a hole anywhere else. Do not put the backend on a network, and do not connect this build to a public game service.

The game log, the captures, and the database have your full Steam id. Keep them out of bug reports and out of the repository.
